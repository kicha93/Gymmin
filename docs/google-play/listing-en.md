## Full description

Gymmin is a free, private workout log that works without an account or server. Your workout data stays on your device and remains under your control.

With Gymmin you can:

• create and edit your own workout plans,
• use an exercise catalog with clear movement previews,
• follow workouts step by step,
• combine exercises into supersets,
• record weight and repetitions,
• review workout history and progress,
• organize a weekly workout plan,
• schedule local reminders,
• export workouts and create local backups,
• prepare plans with an external AI of your choice using a safe copy-and-paste workflow.

Private by design

Gymmin requires no registration and does not synchronize data with a server. Workouts, history, settings, profile, and avatar are stored locally. The app contains no ads or paid features.

AI under your control

The app sends no requests to OpenAI or any other model. Gymmin prepares a prompt locally, while you choose the external service and decide what to share with it.

Safe data backups

You can export a `.gymmin.json` backup, restore it on your device, or irreversibly delete all app data from Settings.

Gymmin is an independent app created and maintained by Paweł Kaliszewski.

Notice: the app is intended for workout planning and education. It does not replace medical or physiotherapy advice.
