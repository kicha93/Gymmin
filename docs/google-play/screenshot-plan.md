# Google Play screenshot plan

Screenshots must come from the final production build and show real app UI. Do not use test accounts, personal avatars, notification icons, developer overlays, or malformed/mixed-language state.

## Required capture set

Prepare separate PL and EN sets. Recommended format: portrait 1080 × 1920 PNG or JPEG, at least four screenshots per language.

1. **Home / weekly plan** — active workouts and weekly progress.
2. **Workout library** — search and workout list with realistic sample content.
3. **Workout details** — notes, exercise overview, and workout actions.
4. **Guided workout** — weight/repetition fields and rest information.
5. **Superset** — two exercises and the round-entry layout.
6. **Progress/history** — completed sessions and progress view.
7. **Exercise details** — START/END movement media.
8. **Local privacy** — Settings → Local data with backup and delete controls.

## Capture rules

- use the final signed/internal-test build;
- use a clean 9:16 phone profile;
- keep system language consistent with the listing locale;
- remove unrelated notifications and personal status-bar details;
- use a neutral local avatar or the default avatar;
- show realistic but non-personal sample workouts;
- do not claim cloud sync, accounts, online AI, premium features, or medical outcomes;
- do not add ranking, pricing, discount, or Google Play badges;
- verify every screenshot still matches the submitted build.
