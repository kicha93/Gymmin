Gymmin Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Gymmin is an independent app created by Paweł Kaliszewski. Contact: kontakt@gymmin.app.

Local data

Workouts, history, settings, weekly plan, reminders, achievements, profile, and avatar are stored only in the app's private storage. Gymmin requires no account and has no server that stores this data.

AI

Gymmin prepares prompts locally and calls neither OpenAI nor any other AI API. The user chooses an external service and explicitly copies content to it; that service's privacy policy then applies.

Explicit external actions

Data can leave the app only after an explicit user action: export or backup, pasting content into another app, or sending a report with the email client. Buy Me a Coffee opens externally; Gymmin sends no data there and support grants no premium functionality.

Bug reports

The prepared email may contain the description, app and OS versions, platform, device model, language, current screen, and at most 10 safe diagnostic events. It excludes workouts, history, weights, creator profiles, AI prompts and responses, tokens, and former account identifiers.

Deletion and backups

Settings lets the user create a local .gymmin.json backup or irreversibly delete all Gymmin data, including avatar, reminders, and transient AI drafts. Static app assets remain unchanged.
